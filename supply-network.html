A Levels

We qualify the network by level, not by listing.

A source only enters the pool once it has cleared the level above it. That is the difference between a network and a directory.

TIER 1

Direct manufacture

Franchised lines and the plants that actually cut, mould or fab the part. Audited on site, traceable to lot, contracted directly.

Full traceability · PPAP available
TIER 2

Qualified partner

Authorised distribution and long-standing partners holding certified stock, used where allocation or MOQ makes direct impractical.

Certificate of conformance · lot-tracked
TIER 3

Screened open market

Used only for obsolete and allocation-constrained lines, and only with incoming inspection and counterfeit screening attached.

Inspection report mandatory
